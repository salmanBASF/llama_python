The Galaxy AI in each unique foldable device is a formidable driving force for everyday needs.

Launching in the romantic city of Paris, Samsung is taking their Galaxy Z lineup to the next level.

# Samsung Unpacked 2024


Samsung has just launched the new Galaxy Z Fold6 and Flip6, along with other products such as the Watch7, Watch Ultra, and more at Samsung Unpacked 2024.

With the new Galaxy Z series, Samsung is reaching new heights by using their most versatile and flexible designs yet to create unique mobile experiences.

Whether users are enjoying the big screen on the Galaxy Z Fold, the FlexWindow on the Galaxy Z Flip, or the handy FlexMode, the Galaxy Z Fold6 and Flip6 will offer even more ways to make the most of AI.

Building on Samsung's legacy of innovative designs, Galaxy AI combines smart, powerful, and durable foldable tech to boost a new era of communication, productivity, and creativity.

The Galaxy Z Fold6 takes productivity and entertainment to new heights.
---
# Galaxy Z Fold6 Transforms Work, Play, and Information Sharing


The Galaxy Z Fold6 transforms the way users work, play, and stay informed, thanks to Samsung's partnership with Google. The new Galaxy Z series comes with the latest Google Gemini app, giving users an AI-powered assistant right on their phone.

Samsung Notes now features Note Assist, which offers translation, summaries, and auto-formatting for easy meeting notes. It also includes a transcript feature that allows users to transcribe, translate, and summarize voice recordings directly in Notes. Users can translate text in PDF files and overlay the translations seamlessly with the PDF overlay translation feature, which also works for text in images and graphs.
---
# Samsung Keyboard Composer and S Pen Enhancements on Galaxy Z Fold6

# The new Composer feature in Samsung Keyboard suggests text based on simple keywords for emails and social media apps. For social media, Composer creates text that matches the user's tone by analyzing their previous posts.

The S Pen experience is enhanced on the Galaxy Z Fold6's screen with Galaxy AI. The new Sketch to Image feature allows users to create sophisticated art by generating image options from their sketches or drawings in the Gallery or Note screen.

The Galaxy Z Flip6 focuses on portability and maximized customization.
---
# Samsung

# The 3.4-inch Super AMOLED FlexWindow has been upgraded, enabling AI-assisted functions without even opening the device. Users can reply to texts with Suggested replies, which analyze their messages to suggest a personalized response.

FlexWindow also provides access to Samsung Health updates and notifications, as well as lets users select the next track on their music widget. Plus, FlexWindow now offers more widgets, allowing users to check information on multiple widgets at any moment.

With the AI-powered Photo Ambient, wallpapers can change in real-time depending on the time and weather. Users can also create a unified look with suggested screen layout options that analyze their wallpaper, move the clock, and change the frame color to match the background image.

FlexWindow isn't the only place for creativity. FlexCam also offers a versatile camera experience with new creative options. The new Auto Zoom feature automatically finds the best framing for still shots by detecting the subject and adjusting the zoom, allowing users to capture precious moments in a snap.
---
# Samsung

# The new 50MP Wide and 12MP Ultra-wide shooters make photography an enjoyable experience.

The 50MP sensor supports 2x optical zoom for noise-free photos and AI zoom for up to 10x zoom.

Nightography, enhanced with video HDR, lets users capture brilliant videos in dim lighting and upload them immediately to popular social apps. The night capturing feature is now available in-app on Instagram, so users can take amazing night photos and share them directly within the app.

# Samsung Galaxy Buds3 Pro: A Premium Audio Experience

Samsung did not forget the audio enthusiasts: The Galaxy Buds3 Pro offers a premium audio experience in a clean package.
---
# Galaxy Buds3 Pro Features


The Galaxy Buds3 Pro features enhanced two-way speakers with a planar tweeter for precise high-range sound and Dual Amplifiers for crystal-clear audio on the go.

With Ultra High Quality Audio and SSC codec, these earbuds support double the sampling rate, letting users enjoy high-resolution audio in superb detail. This exceptional sound quality also extends to phone calls.

Using a pre-trained machine learning model, the Galaxy Buds3 series can restore the speaker's original voice in noisy environments and provide rich, natural audio similar to high-quality smartphone calls, thanks to the Super-Wideband Call feature.
---
# Pre-order Options for Samsung Z Fold6, Z Flip6, and Buds3 Pro


You can already pre-order the Z Fold6, Z Flip6, and Buds3 Pro. The Galaxy Z Fold6 comes in Silver Shadow, Pink, and Navy, while the Galaxy Z Flip6 offers additional color options in Silver Shadow, Yellow, Blue, and Mint.

For even more choices, customers can order online at Samsung's website to access exclusive Galaxy Z series colors like Crafted Black, White, and Peach for the Galaxy Z Flip6.

The Galaxy Buds3 series features a sleek, modern, and comfortable design, available in Silver and White.
