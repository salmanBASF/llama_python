New Year, New Facelift: 2025 Proton X70 Now Open For Booking

The new Proton X70 looks ready to conquer the streets.

The much-anticipated 2025 Proton X70 is now available for booking

Image via Proton (Provided to SAYS)

Proton has unveiled the updated version of its first SUV, featuring a range of enhancements designed to boost both its style and performance.

Here's what's new in the 2025 Proton X70:

Image via Proton (Provided to SAYS)

The updated Proton X70 now features a bold five-point grille with the Infinite Weave design, sleek full-LED reflector headlights, a redesigned front bumper, and new 19-inch alloy wheels.

Inside, the X70 is refreshed with luxurious stone grey Nappa leather seats and multicolour ambient lighting for a sophisticated touch.

Image via Proton (Provided to SAYS)

A major upgrade includes a new 12.3-inch infotainment screen that supports Android Auto and Apple CarPlay. The Proton Link app has also been improved to allow remote engine start, so you can cool down the vehicle before getting in.

The 2025 Proton X70 now features an upgraded ADAS Level 2 system for enhanced safety and convenience, nearly six years after introducing ADAS for premium C-segment SUVs.﻿

Image via Proton (Provided to SAYS)

Bookings for the new Proton X70 are now open

Image via Proton (Provided to SAYS)

With a nominal booking deposit of RM500, you can secure your 2025 Proton X70 at your nearest Proton 3S and 4S showroom or your trusted Proton sales advisor.

Full pricing details have not yet been released, but Proton assures that the price will be attractive and come with early-bird packages.
